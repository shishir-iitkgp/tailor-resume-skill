# Job search preferences

Copy this file to `preferences.md` in the skill folder (next to `SKILL.md`) and fill in your own values.

## Target titles / seniority
e.g. "Director Product, Head of Product, VP Product, Chief Product Officer, CEO - or close variants of these."

**Company-specific leveling equivalence (important - check before scoring a title as a downlevel):**
- Title ladders vary a lot between companies. A "Senior PM" at one company can be more senior than a "Director" at another, and vice versa. Add entries here as you learn them, e.g.: "Company X's 'Senior Product Manager' (L6) = Company Y's 'Director, Product Management' in scope/seniority - not a downlevel."
- If scoring a JD from a company not listed here and the title looks like a downlevel, flag it as a judgment call in `score.md` rather than auto-penalizing, or ask directly.

## Domain preference
e.g. "Strong preference for AI/ML-related roles. Not a hard requirement, but weighted positively in scoring." Leave blank or write "none" if you don't have one.

## Compensation floor
e.g. "$180,000 total compensation (TC). If a JD discloses a range clearly below this, flag as a hard miss. If comp isn't disclosed, mark as 'unknown' - don't penalize the score."

## Tone for cover letters / InMail
e.g. "Concise and direct. No flowery narrative framing." or "Warm and conversational." - whatever matches how you actually want to sound.
