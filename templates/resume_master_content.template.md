# Master Resume Content Bank

Copy this file to `base_resume/resume_master_content.md` and fill it in from your own actual resume(s). This is the **only** source of truth for resume content - tailoring means selecting, reordering, re-prioritizing, and rewording these existing points to match a JD, never inventing new claims, metrics, or tools. The structure below is a suggestion; adapt the sections to however your career actually breaks down, but keep the underlying discipline: nothing goes into a tailored resume that isn't grounded here.

## How this file grows over time

When you mention a real, verifiable accomplishment that isn't here yet (during a conversation about a specific job, not just in the abstract), the skill should ask clarifying questions to pin down the precise, honest scope of the claim, then add it here with an inline note like "(confirmed directly by [you], [date], not from the original resume)" so the provenance is always traceable. If you don't want something added here permanently (e.g. a side project you don't want to formalize into your job-search identity), say so and it'll be used for that one application only, not saved.
